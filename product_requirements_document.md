# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## ASSET PROPERTIES — Private Real Estate Advisory Platform (`@asset_properties17`)
**Author:** Principal AI Product Architect & Writing Coach  
**Target Market:** Bhubaneswar, Odisha (Patia, Infocity, Pahala, Hanspal, Trisulia, Saheed Nagar, Kalinga Nagar, Jayadev Vihar)  
**Core Positioning:** A digital private wealth sanctuary auditing, rejecting, and curating ultra-luxury flats, landed duplex villas, and Grade-A commercial spaces.

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Vision
The real estate market in Bhubaneswar suffers from a deep trust deficit—fear of unverified land titles (*Kissam* issues), inflated super built-up areas, unmapped flood zones, and unorganized broker spam. **Asset Properties** rejects the traditional "listing portal" format in favor of an institutional-grade advisory experience. 

The primary psychological goal is to replace the buyer's natural "broker defense shield" with immediate subconscious trust through weighted motion physics, radical candor (*Curator’s Trade-off Notes*), and rigorous legal verification (*The 100-Point Asset Score™*).

### 2. Target User Personas
1. **The End-Use Luxury Family (Flats & Villas):** Doctors (AIIMS/Apollo/Care), senior IT architects, and established business families seeking community prestige, Vastu compliance, and 100% legal peace of mind.
2. **The Outstation Odia / NRI Investor:** High-earning professionals in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, US, or UAE who need remote ground-reality verification (raw drone footage + Bhulekh/ORERA proof) before deploying ₹1 Cr – ₹5 Cr+.
3. **The Commercial Wealth Builder (Offices):** Chartered Accountants, clinic owners, and IT founders looking to convert monthly office rent into a self-liquidating, tax-efficient commercial asset.
